\subsubsection{Verification -- Hand Calculations}

For a step input voltage $V_s$:

\begin{equation}
V_C(t) = V_s\left(1 - e^{-t/RC}\right)
\end{equation}

Substituting $RC = 0.82\,ms$:

\begin{equation}
V_C(t) = V_s\left(1 - e^{-t/0.82\,ms}\right)
\end{equation}

At one time constant:

\[
V_C(\tau) = V_s(1 - e^{-1}) = 0.632V_s
\]

Hence, the capacitor reaches 63.2\% of its final voltage at
\[
t = 0.82\,ms.
\]
THIS IS FOR THE DISCHARGIN CASE OF STEP SIGNAL
\subsubsection*{Hand Calculation}
When the input is removed:
\begin{equation}
V_C(t) = V_0 e^{-t/RC}
\end{equation}
Substituting $RC = 0.82\,ms$:
\begin{equation}
V_C(t) = V_0 e^{-t/0.82ms}
\end{equation}
At $t = \tau$:
\[
V_C = 0.368V_0
\]
At $t = 5\tau = 4.1\,ms$, the capacitor is almost fully discharged.
