\subsubsection*{Hand Calculation}
When the input is removed:
\begin{equation}
V_C(t) = V_0 e^{-t/RC}
\end{equation}
Substituting $RC = 0.82\,ms$:
\begin{equation}
V_C(t) = V_0 e^{-t/0.82ms}
\end{equation}
At $t = \tau$:
\[
V_C = 0.368V_0
\]
At $t = 5\tau = 4.1\,ms$, the capacitor is almost fully discharged.

\subsection*{Question 2: Short pulse ($T_p \ll RC$)}
\subsubsection*{Hand Calculation}
\[
T_p \ll 0.82\,ms
\]
Peak capacitor voltage:
\begin{equation}
V_{max} = V_s\left(1 - e^{-T_p/RC}\right)
\end{equation}
For $T_p \ll RC$:
\[
V_{max} \approx \frac{T_p}{RC}V_s
